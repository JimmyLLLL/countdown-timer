Countdown Timer

Our free countdown timer lets you count down to any date or event in real time. See exactly how many days, hours, minutes, and seconds remain until your special moment.

Popular Countdowns

  • New Year — Count down to January 1st and ring in the new year!
  • Christmas — How many days until Christmas? Find out instantly.
  • Halloween — Count down to October 31st for spooky season.
  • Custom events — Birthdays, weddings, exams, vacations — anything!

How to Use

Click a preset button for popular holidays, or select a custom date and enter your event name. The countdown updates every second automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I set a custom countdown date? — Yes, count down to any custom date and time, or choose from presets like New Year and Christmas.
